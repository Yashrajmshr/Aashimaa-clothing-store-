# AASHIMA ERP – Customer User Guide

**Target Audience**: Boutique Patrons and Bridal Wear Clients  
**Portal URL**: `https://aashima.in/track`  

---

## 1. How to Track Your Garment Order

### Method 1: Scan Garment QR Tag
If you received an adhesive barcode claim slip at our 581 Turab Nagar counter:
1. Open your smartphone camera and point it at the QR code on your slip.
2. Tap the link to open your live garment dossier instantly.

### Method 2: Online Booking Lookup
1. Visit `https://aashima.in/track` on any phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Enter your Booking Number (e.g. `ASH-8842` found on your receipt).
3. Enter your 10-digit registered mobile number.
4. Tap **Send Verification OTP**.
5. Enter the 6-digit code received via SMS/WhatsApp and tap **Verify**.

---

## 2. Reading Your Live 5-Stage Artisan Timeline
Your live tracker displays the real-time status of your couture article:
1. **Intake & Inspection**: Barcode tag assigned, condition recorded.
2. **Organic Fabric Care**: pH-balanced mild silk emulsion rinse and particulate removal.
3. **Delicate Zari Polish & Steam**: Temperature-regulated steam finishing signed by Master Ramesh Chandra.
4. **Quality Inspection & Packaging**: Archival heirloom enclosure (Active pulsing node).
5. **Ready for Handover**: Available for trial and pickup at our 581 Turab Nagar counter.

## 3. Accessing Invoices & Bills
- Review your total order amount, advance payment received, and remaining dues.
- Click **Download Official Tax Invoice (PDF)** to download your GST receipt for insurance or personal tax records.
