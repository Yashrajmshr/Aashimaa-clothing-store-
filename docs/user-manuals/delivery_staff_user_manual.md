# AASHIMA ERP – Delivery Personnel User Manual

**Target Audience**: Delivery Drivers, Logistics Personnel, Chauffeurs  
**Platform**: AASHIMA Android Mobile App  

---

## 1. Daily Staging & Dispatch
1. Open the **AASHIMA App** and navigate to **Floor -> Delivery Centre**.
2. Inspect packages staged in `Rack #B-14` or assigned to your name.
3. Verify that all garments associated with the booking are assembled together in protective bridal garment bags.

---

## 2. Dispatching an Order
1. Tap **Dispatch** on the delivery voucher.
2. Select status: `Out for Delivery`.
3. System sends an SMS alert to the customer with your name and contact phone.

---

## 3. Customer Handover & Proof of Delivery (POD)
1. Upon arriving at the customer address, request the customer's verified claim slip or check their name.
2. If remaining balance dues exist:
   - Present the shop UPI QR code or collect cash.
   - Enter collected amount in app to update balance to ₹0.
3. Have the client sign the digital / physical delivery sheet or capture a photo of the handed-over package.
4. Tap **Confirm Handover / Delivered**.
5. Customer receives a delivery confirmation and a link to rate fitting satisfaction.
