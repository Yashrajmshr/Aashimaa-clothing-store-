# AASHIMA ERP – Owner User Manual

**Target Audience**: Business Owner (Aashima Singhal) & General Managers  
**Business Entity**: AASHIMA Women's World & AASHIMA Stitchers 'N' Drapers (581 Turab Nagar)  

---

## 1. Welcome to AASHIMA Enterprise ERP
As the owner, you hold master administrative, operational, and financial control over the entire atelier ecosystem across both business divisions:
1. **AASHIMA Women's World**: Premium bridal wear, heavy lehengas, pure silk sarees, zari polishing, and organic dry cleaning.
2. **AASHIMA Stitchers 'N' Drapers**: Gentlemen bespoke suits, sherwanis, bandhgalas, kurtas, and precision trouser alterations.

---

## 2. Navigating the Executive Dashboard
Upon logging in with your owner credentials:
- **Gross Daybook Position**: Live sum of all cash, UPI, and card collections for the active counter shift.
- **Karkhana Live Pipeline**: Real-time breakdown of garments across workshop wings:
  - Alteration & Karigari
  - Organic Deep Cleaning
  - Steam Polish & Finishing
  - Ready for Trial & Handover
- **Critical Red Flags**: Bridal and urgent orders due within 90 minutes.
- **Counter Cash vs UPI**: Real-time audit of payment splits preventing till discrepancy.

---

## 3. Floor Supervision & Karkhana Pipeline Board
- Switch to **Karkhana Pipeline** tab in the sidebar to view the interactive Kanban board.
- Track individual karigar productivity (e.g. Master Iqbal station vs Master Ramesh Chandra station).
- Reassign karigars or fast-track bridal orders directly from the board.

---

## 4. Financial Audit & GST Compliance
- Access **GST Invoicing & Ledger** to inspect generated tax invoices.
- Download official A4 Tax Invoices with SAC 998822 (Tailoring @ 5% GST) and SAC 999712 (Cleaning @ 18% GST).
- Audit expenses and Karigar advances under the **Expenses & P&L** tab.

---

## 5. Staff Management & Access Controls
- Manage staff logins under **Atelier Settings -> Staff Management**.
- Assign role-based permissions ensuring tailors only see their own department queue, while accountants can audit ledgers.
