# AASHIMA ERP – Staff & Artisan User Manual

**Target Audience**: Master Tailors (Master Iqbal), Finishing Masters (Ramesh Chandra), Workshop Artisans, and Front Desk Operators.  
**Platform**: AASHIMA Android Mobile App / Tablet POS  

---

## 1. Daily Shift Login
1. Open the **AASHIMA App** on the workshop tablet or your Android device.
2. Select your staff account (e.g. `master_iqbal` or `ramesh_chandra`) and enter your password.
3. Your home screen displays your assigned department work queue.

---

## 2. Taking New Counter Orders
1. Tap **New Order** on the counter shortcut row.
2. Select or search customer by phone number.
3. Choose Division: **Women's World** or **Stitchers 'N' Drapers**.
4. Add garments, select requested services, and take intake photos of pre-existing wear.
5. If bespoke tailoring is required, open **Fitting Book** to record measurements with 0.25" precision.
6. Enter collected advance and tap **Generate QR & Save Booking**.

---

## 3. QR Scanning & Stage Progression
1. Tap the center elevated **Scan Tag** button.
2. Point your camera at the garment's 80mm adhesive barcode tag.
3. The app displays the **Garment Dossier** with client name, special instructions, and intake photos.
4. Select the completed stage (e.g. `Finishing`, `Ready for Delivery`).
5. Add floor remarks (e.g., "Steam press completed by Master Ramesh. QC Passed.") and tap **Complete Stage Transition**.
6. The customer's tracking website reflects the update immediately.

---

## 4. Printing Thermal Slips
1. Tap **Print Slip** from any order or garment dossier.
2. Verify paired Bluetooth printer (`00:11:22:33:44:55`).
3. Tap **Print** to output the 80mm adhesive job card and perforated claim slip.
4. Staple the tag firmly to the garment hanger.
